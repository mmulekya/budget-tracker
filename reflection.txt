MongoDB felt easiest for storing the student record because I could insert the document directly without defining a table structure first. PostgreSQL required me to define the structure upfront by creating the students table with specific columns and data types. MongoDB allowed me to insert data without a predefined structure, while Redis stores information as simple key-value pairs.
